    Instructions & Troubleshooting

    How to Use

    1. Connect your device to the same Wi-Fi / local network as your Klipper printers.
    2. MoonScout will automatically estimate your subnet prefix (e.g., 192.168.1). Adjust it manually if needed.
    3. Tap Scout to scan IPs .1 through .254 on port 7125.

    Install as a Mobile App

    • iOS (Safari): Tap the Share button → Add to Home Screen.
    • Android (Chrome): Tap the 3 dots → Add to Home Screen / Install App.

    Troubleshooting (No printers found?)

    • Wi-Fi Network: Ensure your phone or browser isn't connected to a Guest Wi-Fi network or cellular data.
    • Moonraker CORS Config: Ensure your printers' moonraker.conf allows cross-origin requests: 
      [authorization]
cors_domains:
    *
