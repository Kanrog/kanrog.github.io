    Instructions & Finding Your Subnet

    How to Use

    1. Ensure your device is connected to your home Wi-Fi / local network.
    2. MoonScout will attempt to auto-detect your subnet. Tap Scout to begin scanning.
    3. Discovered printers will list their IP address along with their Moonraker/Klipper hostname.

    How to Find Your Subnet Prefix

    Your subnet prefix consists of the first 3 numbers of your IP address (e.g., if your device IP is 192.168.0.45, your subnet is 192.168.0):

    • Windows: Command Prompt → ipconfig
    • macOS: System Settings → Network → Wi-Fi → Details
    • iOS: Settings → Wi-Fi → tap the info icon (i) next to network
    • Android: Settings → Network & internet → Wi-Fi → tap gear icon

    Moonraker Configuration Check

    Ensure your printer's moonraker.conf accepts cross-origin API calls and listens on all interfaces:

    [server]
host: 0.0.0.0
port: 7125

[authorization]
cors_domains:
    *

    How It Works (Privacy & Security)

    100% Client-Side & Private

    MoonScout runs entirely in your web browser. No telemetry, analytics, tracking, or local IP details are ever transmitted to any external server.

    Port 7125 Name Extraction & Batched Verification

    MoonScout issues queries to port 7125 in controlled batches of 10. When accessible, MoonScout extracts the hostname directly from Moonraker's /server/info endpoint, falling back to socket verification if cross-origin rules restrict JSON body access.
